Blog

Write a blog post

Share an idea you're working on, a paper you liked, or something you've learned this year. Fill out the form and we'll put it on the blog.

What to write about

  • Research you're working on: a question you're exploring, an early result, or a problem you're stuck on.
  • A paper review: a paper you've read closely, what it does, and where you'd push back.
  • Something useful you've learned: a coding trick, a proof technique, or a way into a literature.
  • Notes from a talk or conference: the papers presented at a Berkeley seminar or a conference you attended, and what you took from them. Keep it about the research.
  • A dataset worth knowing about: what's in it, how to get access, and what questions it could answer.
  • A method explained: an estimator, an identification strategy, or a numerical method, with the intuition first.
  • An open question: a puzzle you think deserves a paper, and why existing work doesn't settle it.

Guidelines and rules

  • Who you're writing for: PhD students across Econ, ARE, and Haas, not just your own field. Explain terms and notation that others might not know.
  • Length: up to 1,000 words. Math and links don't count toward the limit.
  • Math: you can include it, but use it only where it's needed. Write it in LaTeX, with $…$ for inline math and $$…$$ for a displayed equation, for example $\beta R$ or $$u'(c_t) = \beta R\, E_t[u'(c_{t+1})]$$.
  • Links: put the link in square brackets right after what it refers to, for example As Acemoglu (2025) [link: https://…] shows. The reference becomes the link.
  • Proofread first: posts go up as written, so make sure yours is polished before you submit.
  • Image: every post needs one. It goes at the top and is the post's thumbnail, and it can be decorative, as long as it relates to your topic. Use your own photo or research figure, or an image licensed for reuse (Unsplash, Pexels, CC0 or public domain, or CC BY with credit).
  • Not allowed: images from Google Images or news sites, figures from published journal articles, and logos.
  • Copyright: you keep the copyright to your post. By submitting, you let us publish it on this site and confirm you have the right to share everything in it, including the image. You're responsible for any copyright or other problems with what you submit.

To request removal of an image, email berkeleyfirstyearworkshop@gmail.com.
